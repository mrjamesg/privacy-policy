Privacy Policy The Cipher Gazette — Last updated: August 16, 2026 This Privacy Policy describes how The Cipher Gazette ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use the app. Summary: The Cipher Gazette does not require you to create an account and does not collect your name, email, or other personal information directly. The app stores your game progress locally on your device. The app shows ads and offers one optional in-app purchase, both handled by third-party services (Google AdMob and your device's app store) that may collect limited technical data as described below. Information We Do Not Collect We do not require account creation, and we do not directly collect your name, email address, physical address, or other personally identifiable information. Any player name you enter in the app's Profile screen is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to us. Information Stored on Your Device The app saves your game progress — puzzles solved, streaks, hints used, achievements, and settings — locally on your device using the platform's standard save-game storage. This data is not transmitted to us and is deleted if you uninstall the app or use the app's "Reset All Progress" option. Third-Party Services The app uses the following third-party services, each of which operates under its own privacy policy: Google AdMob — displays advertising within the app. AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as your advertising ID), general location (derived from IP address), and app usage data to serve and measure ads. See Google's Privacy Policy. Google Play Billing / Apple In-App Purchase — processes the app's one optional in-app purchase ("Remove Ads"). Payment information is handled entirely by the app store (Google Play or the Apple App Store), not by us — we never see or store your payment details. Device app store review prompts — the app may ask you to rate it using the operating system's built-in review prompt (Google Play In-App Review / Apple's native review prompt). This interaction is handled entirely by the platform. Notifications If you enable "Daily Reminders" in Settings, the app schedules a local notification on your device to remind you about the daily puzzle. This is a device-local notification — no data about you is sent to us or to any third party to make this happen, and it does not use push-notification servers. Children's Privacy The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Data Retention & Deletion Since your progress is stored only on your device, you can delete all app data at any time by using the "Reset All Progress" option in Settings, or by uninstalling the app. Changes to This Policy We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
